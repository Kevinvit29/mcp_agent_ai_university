University Student Data and AI Chatbot Policy

1. The AI chatbot may answer public questions about university programs, admission requirements, tuition fees, and general policies.
2. Student records are considered personal data and must be protected according to PDPA principles.
3. PDPA does not prevent all database access. It requires lawful, limited, necessary, and secure access.
4. Public users cannot access individual student records.
5. Advisors may access academic information such as student ID, name, program, GPA, academic status, and advisor notes for educational support.
6. Sensitive fields such as email, phone number, national ID, passport ID, and address must be masked unless the requester is an authorized admin.
7. Admin users may access full records only for legitimate administrative purposes.
8. The LLM must not directly query MongoDB or PostgreSQL.
9. All database requests must go through the MCP Server gateway and predefined tools.
10. The system should keep audit logs in production.
